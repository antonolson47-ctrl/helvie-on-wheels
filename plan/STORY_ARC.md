# HELVIE ON WHEELS — Story Arc & Game Plan (for approval; nothing built yet)

**Logline:** A grinning retiree in a ST. GERMAIN cap pulls the most polite-yet-explosive heist in Pacific Northwest history: $12 million in bearer bonds, one parachute, two 18-wheelers, a mine-dropping speedboat, his rocket-happy wife Julia, and a mustache-twirling dictator who put his flag on *everything*.

**Tone:** South Park–style 18+ cartoon mayhem; GTA loading-screen art; 1960s Batman-TV comic bursts (KA-BLAM! POW! ZOWIE! + Canadian EH-BOOM!, SORRY-SPLAT!, TOQUE-POW!). Running gag: Americans arrive with absurd, unlimited firepower; Canadians apologize while getting blown up.
**Priorities (per Anton):** iPhone/iPad Safari first; story richness over challenge (Easy/forgiving default, no permanent fail: the **"Jesus, take the wheel!"** rescue replaces the plain rewind (see below), "Story Mode" on by default); satisfying payoffs every level.

## Cast
- **ANDY HELVIE** – hero. Photo likeness (warm grin, crow's feet, dark ST. GERMAIN cap). Calm, folksy, unbothered. Catchphrases: "Well, that escalated nicely." / "Keep the change, Uncle Sam." / "Bonds. Andy Bonds." / "I'm retired. This is my hobby."
- **JULIA HELVIE** – wife, joins in Vancouver. *Placeholder look* (replace with photo): silver bob with hot-pink streak, black shades, leather jacket, red lipstick, pink boots, bazooka labeled "∞ UNLIMITED." Catchphrases: "Honey, I packed snacks and rockets." / "Make it rain, sweetheart." / "Sorry? I'm not sorry. I'm from Ohio." / "Reload? I don't know her."
- **BOB** – the Helvies' orange tabby. Rides along from Vancouver onward (Julia brings both cats in the pink minivan, in a carrier labeled "EMOTIONAL SUPPORT ATTACK CATS"). Pounces on Mounties' faces, shreds tires and hats. Burst: HISS-POW!
- **JAKE** – grey kitty, the sniper of the duo. Lobs **hairball bombs** (EH-BOOM! / SORRY-SPLAT!) from the trailer roof, knocks donuts off Mountie dashboards. Mounties: "Sorry! Nice kitty, eh?!"
- **Sgt. Dewey Puddlesworth** – Seattle-ish PD, chases Andy all game, keeps filing paperwork mid-chase.
- **Constable Bryce McPoliteson, RCMP** – rides a moose named Gordie. "Stop, or I'll say stop again!"
- **GENERALISSIMO JOSE WIDEN** – original fictional dictator of **PROPAGANDIA** (purple-over-gold flag with a megaphone emblem). Gigantic handlebar mustache (it has its own medal), tall purple shako with red plume, monocle, 47 medals including "Best Medal." Flies the flag on everything: helicopters, cereal, Andy's cereal. Pursues Andy because the bonds were stolen *from his offshore account*. Lines: "Every flag is MY flag!" / "Nooo! My second-best mustache!"

## Bob & Jake in gameplay (Level 5 onward)
- **Level 5 shootout:** CAT button (cooldown 8 s). Bob pounces on the nearest Mountie (stun + HISS-POW!), Jake lobs a hairball bomb (area knockback, SORRY-SPLAT!). Mounties stop to apologize to the cats.
- **Levels 6-7 (semi):** the cats ride the trailer roof next to Julia. Bob leaps onto chasing cruisers and shreds tires (they spin out). Jake hairball-bombs roadblocks. Tap a cat portrait to fire.
- **Finale:** both cats on the summit. Phase 2: Jake hairballs the megaphone drones. Final cutscene: Bob bats the Generalissimo's falling mustache like a toy.
- Gags: the cats refuse to wear toques; a Mountie tries to give Jake a ticket and gets a hairball instead; victory screen shows "Cats: 47, Mounties: Sorry."

## Rescue mechanic: "JESUS, TAKE THE WHEEL!" (replaces the plain rewind)
Triggers in any driving/racing level (semi, speedboat, Trans-Canada run) when a fail is coming: a crash off the road or cliff, health near zero, or falling too far behind.
1. Time goes into slow-mo and Andy shouts his catchphrase, **"JESUS, TAKE THE WHEEL!"** (Julia sometimes adds "Amen, and step on it!").
2. A **golden light beam** shines down from the clouds, with sparkles and floating glowing music notes. An original **angelic choir sting** plays ("AAAAAH-MEN!").
3. A slow-mo comic burst pops: **HALLELU-WHOOSH!** / **HOLY-MOLY!** / **AMEN-BAM!**
4. The rig or boat floats up out of danger and gets set back down gently on the right line, facing the right way, with a little health topped up. The cops chasing them keep crashing into the canyon.
- No figure is shown, just the warm light. The tone is affectionate, never mocking. Unlimited uses on Easy, a light time/star penalty only (stars are never needed to progress). Running gags: Andy tips his cap to the sky ("Much obliged"); a Mountie sees it and apologizes to the heavens; on the 3rd rescue in a level Andy says "Sorry to bother You again."

## Controls (phone/iPad, portrait & landscape)
- **Semi & boat:** left thumb drag = steer (or tilt option); auto-gas with BRAKE button; right side = BOOST. Wide forgiving roads, rubber-band pursuers, auto-steer assist on Easy.
- **Shootouts:** auto-aim/auto-fire; left joystick moves/takes cover; big SPECIAL button (rocket/airstrike); tap barrels/pumps to detonate.
- **Boat mines:** MINE button (unlimited, 1 s cooldown).
- **Rockets (Race 3):** Julia auto-fires; tap any pursuer to target; SPECIAL for salvo.
- iPad: larger HUD, side panels show dialogue/portraits; iPhone: compact HUD, safe-area aware. Pinch/double-tap zoom disabled; RESET VIEW button.

## Destruction set pieces
Exploding espresso kiosk row (Seattle-ish), Gas 'n' Gulp pump chain reaction, ferry nudge into a seafood restaurant, flaming Zamboni, Tom Hartons drive-thru donut tsunami, maple syrup tanker spill (everyone slides), puck avalanche, gold helicopter crash.

## Score / currency
**Bonds** (each worth $100K) collected as currency + score. Spend at **"Uncle Sam's Pawn & Pew"** between levels for upgrades (fire rate, rocket size, truck armor, boat mines). Stars (1-3) per level for style, never for basic progress.

## Victory payoffs (every level)
Slow-mo finishing blow → giant comic burst → original victory fanfare sting → cash-and-bonds shower → Andy/Julia quip → medal/star screen with stats (cops embarrassed, apologies received, pumps detonated). Finale: fireworks, mustache feather fall, credits with dancing moose and a hockey-organ remix.
