# Helvie on Wheels: quick summary
Andy (in his ST. GERMAIN cap) hijacks a jet over "Seattle-ish," grabs $12 million in bearer bonds and parachutes out. He steals a semi and outruns the cops to a marina. There he blows up the Gas 'n' Gulp pumps and takes off in the BOND VOYAGE speedboat, dropping silly mines all the way to Vancouver. Julia shows up there with unlimited rockets. The blood runs like maple syrup ("Grade A. Dark."), mounties ride moose, and everyone says sorry. They grab a second semi for the Trans-Canada run: Andy drives and Julia blows everything up. It ends on Mustache Mountain, where they shoot down Generalissimo Jose Widen's gold helicopter, PROPAGANDIA ONE.

- 8 levels plus a finale: 3 driving races (semi, boat, semi with rockets), 2 big shootouts, a freefall opener and the mountain boss.
- It's easy and forgiving, so the story comes first. When a race is about to go wrong (crash, low health, falling behind), Andy yells "JESUS, TAKE THE WHEEL!", a golden beam of light and an angel choir lift the rig back onto the road (HALLELU-WHOOSH!), and you keep going. There are lots of cutscenes and jokes.
- BOB (orange tabby) and JAKE (grey kitty) join in Vancouver: Bob pounces on Mounties' faces and shreds tires, and Jake throws hairball bombs. The Mounties apologize to the cats.
- The art is a 3D toy-brick (BRIKS) minifig style: 7 panels plus a title card in plan/panels/.
- The Americans have a huge, ridiculous, unlimited arsenal (Eagle Screamer 9000, Freedom Shotgun, Liberty Mines). The Canadians fight back with hockey sticks and strongly worded letters.
- Batman-style comic bursts pop up everywhere (KA-BLAM!, POW!, EH-BOOM!, SORRY-SPLAT!).
- Every win ends with a slow-mo finish, a bonds shower, a victory fanfare and a star screen.
- All the music is original spy-thriller surf guitar and brass. It's built for iPhone and iPad first.
